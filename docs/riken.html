Statement to RIKEN Employees Who Use or Contribute to This Project

This project is openly available to everyone and is distributed under the CPAL-1.0. Its purpose as an open-source project is to make knowledge, tools, and technology freely available for people to use, study, modify, and share, and to enable researchers working in different institutions, regions, and positions to participate collectively in the production of knowledge.

However, the author does not believe that open source requires the author to remain politically neutral toward every person who uses or contributes to this project.

Scientific research does not exist outside social relations. Laboratories, research institutes, and universities are workplaces as well as institutions of knowledge. Papers, data, software, and scientific achievements are produced through the collective labor of researchers, technical staff, administrative workers, students, and many others. The so-called “prestige of a research institution” or “status of a researcher” must not become a privilege above other workers, nor should it be used to justify looking down upon researchers from other institutions, legitimizing labor exploitation, or preserving hierarchical relations within scientific institutions.

The author particularly rejects the institutional hierarchy and division of workers that have long existed within scientific research. Some people, simply because they belong to institutions with greater prestige and resources, mistake institutionally granted status for their own personal ability and worth, and use that status to look down upon researchers from other institutions. At the same time, they may accept or actively preserve unequal treatment of postdoctoral researchers, contract researchers, technical staff, administrative workers, students, and other scientific workers.

Such an ideology is not politically neutral. It objectively serves hierarchical relations within the scientific system. It encourages workers to compete against and divide themselves from one another, preventing scientific workers who share common interests from building collective power.



Scientific workers are, first and foremost, workers.



Therefore, the author calls upon researchers at RIKEN to seriously confront the labor relations in which they themselves are situated. Do not place yourself above other researchers simply because of the social status of your institution. Do not transform institutional hierarchy into contempt for other workers. And do not become an instrument for suppressing other workers or undermining workers' solidarity for the sake of personal advancement, institutional interests, or the interests of capital.

The author is particularly opposed to scabbery and to any conduct that seeks personal advantage at the expense of other workers. Whether a researcher deserves respect should not be determined by the name of their institution, but by how they treat the workers with whom they labor.

Therefore, if you are an employee or researcher at RIKEN and choose to stand on the side of managerial power, institutional hierarchy, and the interests of capital, using that position to suppress, divide, or look down upon other scientific workers; if you treat your institutional identity as capital that places you above researchers elsewhere and refuse to recognize the common class interests shared by scientific workers, then:

The author does not welcome you as a contributor to this project, nor does the author wish for you to use this project.


What the author genuinely hopes to see is that workers at RIKEN and other research institutions can move beyond the artificial hierarchy and competition imposed between institutions, recognize their common condition as workers, and stand together with researchers, technical staff, administrative workers, postdoctoral researchers, students, and other workers in the struggle for better working conditions, fair treatment, and the dignity of labor.


Knowledge should be open, and research should be shared. But workers should not be turned against one another.

The value of scientific research should not be determined by capital or institutional hierarchy, and the dignity of scientific workers should not be determined by the name of their institution.

This statement expresses the author's personal political position and value judgments concerning scientific labor, class relations, and workers' solidarity. It is not an additional licensing condition to the CPAL-1.0 and does not modify any rights granted by the CPAL-1.0.
