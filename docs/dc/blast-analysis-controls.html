External References, Family BLAST, and Filtering

External-reference stage

BLAST can independently enable automatic hit Symbol Name identification, UniProt enrichment, and InterPro enrichment. UniProt adds accession/canonical-length and review evidence. InterPro resolves conserved-region evidence through the official EBI API. Missing evidence remains blank; PHgo does not invent a weak mapping. Results are cached and duplicate lookups are coalesced.

InterPro conserved-region defaults

The status evaluator uses Pfam, InterPro and signature accessions, entry type, coverage, and match regions by default; entry-name matching is off. present requires at least 70% coverage and one matched item, while partial starts at 25% coverage and one matched item. These thresholds are editable in the external-reference window.

Family BLAST

When at least two query labels share a detectable family pattern, PHgo offers Family BLAST. Default behavior groups by detected prefix, strips leading species text, trailing member indexes, number-following suffixes and terminal subtype suffixes, normalizes inner punctuation, then merges duplicate target rows and keeps the preferred hit. Minimum group size is 2. Grouping and merging are separate: turning merging off keeps the family unit but retains all member hit rows. The derived family_name does not rewrite hit label_name.

When Filter is available

The automatic BLAST Filter appears only when both UniProt and InterPro reference columns are present for the run. Applying it changes suggested checkbox state and marks suggested removals; it does not delete rows. Manual checkbox changes remain possible, and rerunning the filter restores its current recommendation.

Default hard decisions

  • Identity, query coverage, and E-value limits default to 0/off.
  • Canonical target length is required and accepted at 70–130% when that evidence exists for the program.
  • InterPro conserved region is required; present and partial are accepted, missing and uncertain are rejected.
  • UniProt accession and reviewed status are not hard requirements by default.
  • The best isoform per target gene is retained; top-N limiting is off.
  • Soft scoring is off. Ranking still uses score, identity, coverage, reference evidence, E-value, then bitscore as configured tie-breakers.

Strong evidence fallback

For rows missing reference anchors, the default fallback may retain a very strong BLAST result: identity at least 40%, E-value at most 1e-80, and target/query length 80–120%. Family-consensus and semantic-agreement requirements are separately configurable. Review these rules before applying them to nucleotide/genome-target programs, where protein canonical-length evidence may not be biologically equivalent.

Recommended use

Run once without aggressive optional identity/E-value rules, inspect why rows were rejected, then tighten thresholds for the scientific question. Because Filter changes selection suggestions rather than deleting evidence, retain XLSX or PGO if auditability matters.
